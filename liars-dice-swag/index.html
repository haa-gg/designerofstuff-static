Liar’s Dice Swag

So I submitted my game to get a booth at a local tech conference at the Best Buy headquarters and scored a spot!

Want the technical details on the game? Go here.

Knowing a thing or two about running booths I spent the following 7 days rapidly designing and fabricating the following…

A “Quest” Board Laser-Cut By Yours Truly

That explains to the people visiting my booth how to play my game and earn an award for their interest.

Custom Reward Cards

That I designed, etched and cut all solo dolo with unique QR codes that download the android version of my app and remove the ad banner from it (will cook up a set of these for ios soon)

The cards went into these sweet envelopes

Hand-sealed with sealing wax… Fun fact this wax is quite flammable!

The finished product looked something like this

The combination of custom loot, swag, and signage were an absolute hit! I had tons of people coming up to the booth, playing my game and giving feedback a million times more thoughtful than I was expecting.
